## 🎨 Brand Usage Tips

### Logo
- Use on white or light backgrounds primarily
- Maintain clear space around logo
- Don't stretch or distort
- Use provided color variations

### Colors
- Navy Blue: Trust, professionalism, stability
- Accent Blue: Innovation, technology
- Success Green: Growth, results, achievement

### Messaging
- Always emphasize **precision** and **navigation**
- Focus on **measurable outcomes**
- Highlight **strategic** approach
- Use **data-backed** claims

### Tone
- Professional but approachable
- Confident without arrogance
- Strategic and thoughtful
- Clear and direct
