## 📈 **Phase 2: Direct Outreach (Week 2-4)**

### **2.1 Target Customer Identification**

#### **Primary Targets:**
- **Mid-Market Companies** (50-500 employees)
- **Data-Rich Industries**: E-commerce, SaaS, Healthcare, Manufacturing
- **Pain Points**: Manual processes, data silos, slow decision-making

#### **Ideal Customer Profile:**
- **Role**: CTO, CDO, VP of Operations, Data Managers
- **Company Size**: 50-500 employees
- **Revenue**: $5M-$50M annually
- **Current State**: Using basic analytics, manual reporting
- **Growth Stage**: Scaling and need efficiency

### **2.2 Outreach Strategy**

#### **LinkedIn Approach:**
```
Subject: The V> Method for [Company] - 30% efficiency boost in 90 days

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] is growing rapidly. Most companies at your stage struggle with manual data processes that slow down decision-making.

We help companies like yours embed AI directly into workflows (not just add analytics on top).

The result? 30% faster decisions, 40% less manual work, and data that actually drives growth.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see how the V> approach could work for [specific pain point]?

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, Via New
```

#### **Email Sequence:**
- **Email 1**: Introduction + free audit offer
- **Email 2**: Case study + specific benefit
- **Email 3**: Social proof + limited-time offer
- **Email 4**: Final value + soft close

### **2.3 Free Consultation Strategy**
- **Offer**: "Free AI-First Business Audit"
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Deliverable**: 3 specific recommendations
- **Follow-up**: Custom proposal within 48 hours

- **Free Audit**: $0 (lead generation)
- **Quick Win Package**: $5,000 (30-day AI workflow optimization)
