# Via New - Brand Guidelines

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** Via New  
**Tagline:** "Delivering value via new path"  
**Full Description:** "Data driven and AI embedded in process workflows"

## Brand Positioning

Via New is positioned as an innovative technology company that delivers value through new, intelligent pathways. We specialize in integrating AI and data-driven solutions directly into business process workflows, creating seamless automation and intelligent decision-making systems.

## Core Brand Values

### 1. Innovation Through New Paths
- We don't follow traditional approaches
- We create new, more efficient ways to solve problems
- We embrace cutting-edge AI and automation technologies

### 2. Data-Driven Intelligence
- Every solution is backed by data insights
- We embed intelligence directly into workflows
- Decisions are made based on real-time data analysis

### 3. Process Integration
- AI is not an add-on, it's embedded in the core processes
- Solutions work seamlessly within existing workflows
- We optimize the entire process, not just individual components

### 4. Measurable Value Delivery
- Every solution delivers quantifiable business outcomes
- We focus on ROI and measurable improvements
- Growth is data-driven and sustainable

## Visual Identity

### Logo
- **Primary Logo:** "V" in a gradient circle (primary-500 to accent-500)
- **Typography:** Bold, modern sans-serif
- **Tagline:** "delivering value via new path" (smaller, secondary text)

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Purple gradients (primary-400 to primary-600)
- **Secondary:** Teal/Accent colors (accent-400 to accent-600)
- **Supporting:** Rose, cream, and neutral grays
- **Background:** Clean whites and light grays

### Typography
- **Headings:** Playfair Display (serif) - elegant and authoritative
- **Body Text:** Plus Jakarta Sans (sans-serif) - modern and readable
- **Style:** Clean, minimalist, professional

## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Tone of Voice
- **Innovative:** Forward-thinking and cutting-edge
- **Intelligent:** Data-driven and analytical
- **Confident:** Assured in our solutions and expertise
- **Accessible:** Complex concepts made simple

### Key Messages
1. **"Delivering value via new path"** - Our core differentiator
2. **"Data driven and AI embedded"** - Our technical approach
3. **"Process workflows"** - Where we integrate our solutions
4. **"Intelligent automation"** - What we deliver

### Messaging Framework
- **Problem:** Traditional approaches are outdated and inefficient
- **Solution:** Via New creates intelligent, automated workflows
- **Outcome:** Measurable business growth and operational efficiency

- **Technology Leaders:** CTOs, CDOs, VP of Engineering
- **Business Leaders:** CEOs, COOs, VPs of Operations

- Organizations seeking competitive advantage through AI

3. **New Path Approach:** We create innovative solutions, not incremental improvements
4. **Measurable Outcomes:** Every solution delivers quantifiable business value
