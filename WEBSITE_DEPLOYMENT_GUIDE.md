## 🚀 **How to Add Custom Domain to Vercel**

### **Step 1: Add Domain in Vercel Dashboard**
1. Go to: https://vercel.com/dashboard
2. Click on your project: `ddenterprise`
3. Go to **Settings** → **Domains**
4. Click **Add Domain**
5. Enter your domain (e.g., `vianew.com`)

### **Step 2: Update DNS Records**
Vercel will provide DNS records to add to your domain registrar:

```
Type: A
Name: @
Value: 76.76.19.61

Type: CNAME
Name: www
Value: cname.vercel-dns.com
```

### **Step 3: Wait for SSL**
- SSL certificate will be automatically generated
- Usually takes 5-10 minutes
- Your site will be available at `https://yourdomain.com`
